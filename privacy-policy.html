Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Daisy Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), your privacy is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy universally applies to all mobile games, puzzle titles, and interactive applications published by Daisy Game Studio on the Google Play Store and other mobile distribution platforms.

Summary for Players: Our games do NOT require personal user registration or accounts, do NOT request access to private contacts, GPS location, microphone, or camera, and do NOT sell personal user data to third parties.

1. Scope & Application

This Privacy Policy covers all games and software distributed under the Daisy Game Studio developer account. By installing, updating, or playing any of our titles on your mobile device, you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and usage practices described in this document.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We design our games with a privacy-first architecture, collecting only what is strictly necessary to deliver smooth, crash-free, and engaging entertainment:

  • Local Gameplay Data: High scores, completed levels, achievements, virtual currency, and game settings (audio, theme, haptic feedback) are stored strictly locally on your own device's internal storage.
  • Anonymous Hardware & System Specs: General technical information (device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language preference) may be processed locally by the game engine to optimize rendering and frame rates.

3. Third-Party Services: Firebase & AdMob

To provide high-quality free-to-play games, deliver in-game rewards, and ensure continuous technical stability, our games integrate trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google LLC:

AdvertisingGoogle AdMob:
Used to deliver banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID) and device information to display relevant or contextual ads, track ad performance, and combat ad fraud. Our official authorized publisher verification is hosted at daisygame.github.io/app-ads.txt.

AnalyticsGoogle Firebase Analytics:
Used to collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable telemetry data (e.g., game launch counts, level completion rates, session duration) to help us balance puzzle difficulty and understand player retention without identifying individual players.

Crash ReportingGoogle Firebase Crashlytics:
Used to automatically detect, diagnose, and fix game crashes and technical bugs. When a crash occurs, Crashlytics records stack traces and hardware state at the moment of the crash so our engineering team can swiftly deploy stability patches.

For more details on how Google processes and secures data across these services, please consult:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob Privacy Disclosures
  • Google Firebase Privacy and Security

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Daisy Game Studio is deeply committed to protecting the privacy of children:

  • We adhere strictly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • In games designated for families or children, advertising requests are flagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA / TFCD / TFUA compliance) and restricted to family-safe ad categories.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Ad Controls (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have full control over advertising identifiers:

  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID anytime in your Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete Advertising ID.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can turn off personalized ad recommendations through your device settings or through any in-game consent management dialogs (UMP / GDPR consent forms).

6. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect newly published games, platform feature additions, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
